Healthy Lifestyle

Small daily habits can make a positive difference in your life.

🥗 Balanced Nutrition

Include different food groups in your meals, such as fruits, vegetables, grains, protein-rich foods and dairy or suitable alternatives.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Your water needs may vary depending on the weather, your activity and your health.

🚶 Physical Activity

Walking, cycling, dancing and other enjoyable activities can support physical and mental well-being. Choose activities that suit your abilities.

🌙 Healthy Sleep

Maintain a regular sleep schedule and create a calm sleeping environment. Good-quality sleep supports concentration, mood and general health.
