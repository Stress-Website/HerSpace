Self-Care Corner

Take a little time for yourself. You deserve care and kindness.

🧴 Skincare Basics

A simple skincare routine may include a gentle cleanser, a suitable moisturiser and sunscreen during the day. Choose products that suit your skin.

💆 Hair Care

Use a shampoo suitable for your scalp and conditioner for your hair lengths. Avoid excessive heat and harsh treatments that may damage your hair.

📖 Make Time for Yourself

Reading, drawing, listening to music or spending time outdoors can be enjoyable ways to relax and reconnect with yourself.

🌼 Set Healthy Boundaries

It is okay to say no when you need rest or time for yourself. Healthy boundaries help you balance your responsibilities and personal needs.
